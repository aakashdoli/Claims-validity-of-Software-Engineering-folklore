## Week 1 (Feb 17–Feb 23, 2026)

Objective  
Prepare the extraction pipeline and methodology for a controlled 2-book pilot.

Tasks

- Finalize operational definition of “claim”.
- Write inclusion and exclusion rules with examples.
- Ensure all traceability fields exist in output:
  - book_id
  - chapter_title
  - section_title
  - paragraph_index
  - stable ebook locator
- Prepare manual verification template (30–50 claims sampling).
- Validate pipeline runs end-to-end without structural errors.

Expected Output

- Clean extraction schema.
- Documented claim definition.
- Manual verification template ready.
- Pilot Book 1 ready for execution next week.

Success Criteria

- No structural CSV errors.
- Clear separation between claims and anecdotes.
- Traceability verified manually on sample paragraphs.
